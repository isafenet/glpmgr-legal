Trulicity injection sites: what the manufacturer says

Trulicity (dulaglutide), checked against the documents below on 20 September 2026

This page lists where the manufacturer says Trulicity (dulaglutide) can be injected in the UK, the EU and the US, in their own words, with the documents each list comes from. It is not medical advice.

Talk to a healthcare professional. The injection areas shown come from the manufacturer's product information, and the shading shows where you've injected before. GLPMGR is not a medical device and this is not medical advice: it can't tell you where it is safe for you to inject. Talk to your doctor, pharmacist or nurse about where and how to inject, follow the instructions from your prescriber and the leaflet supplied with your medicine, and ask a healthcare professional if you are unsure, or if you have pain, lumps, bruising or any other problem at an injection site.

Where Trulicity can be injected

Manufacturer: Eli Lilly

United Kingdom

Areas listed:

  • Stomach area (abdomen)
  • Upper leg (thigh)
  • Upper arm — another person should inject here

Rotation, in the manufacturer’s words: “Change (rotate) your injection site each week. You may use the same area of your body, but be sure to choose a different injection site in that area.”

Source: emc / Eli Lilly: Trulicity 4.5 mg pre-filled pen Instructions for Use. Checked 20 September 2026.

European Union

Areas listed:

  • Stomach area (abdomen)
  • Upper leg (thigh)
  • Upper arm — another person should inject here

Rotation, in the manufacturer’s words: “Change (rotate) your injection site each week. You may use the same area of your body, but be sure to choose a different injection site in that area.”

Source: EMA: Trulicity EPAR Product Information (English). Checked 20 September 2026.

United States

Areas listed:

  • Abdomen
  • Thigh
  • Upper arm — another person should inject here

Rotation, in the manufacturer’s words: “Change (rotate) your injection site with each weekly injection.”

Source: FDA / DailyMed: Trulicity prescribing information and patient instructions (DailyMed, current label), revised 11/2024. Checked 20 September 2026.
