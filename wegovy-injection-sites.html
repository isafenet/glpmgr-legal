Wegovy injection sites and dose steps: what the manufacturer says

Wegovy (semaglutide), checked against the documents below on 20 September 2026

This page lists where the manufacturer says Wegovy (semaglutide) can be injected in the UK, the EU and the US, and its dose steps and missed-dose wording, in their own words, with the documents each comes from. It is not medical advice.

Talk to a healthcare professional. The injection areas shown come from the manufacturer's product information, and the shading shows where you've injected before. GLPMGR is not a medical device and this is not medical advice: it can't tell you where it is safe for you to inject. Talk to your doctor, pharmacist or nurse about where and how to inject, follow the instructions from your prescriber and the leaflet supplied with your medicine, and ask a healthcare professional if you are unsure, or if you have pain, lumps, bruising or any other problem at an injection site.

Where Wegovy can be injected

Manufacturer: Novo Nordisk

United Kingdom

Areas listed:

  • Abdomen (Stomach) — keep at least 5 cm from your belly button
  • Thigh (Upper leg)
  • Upper arm

Rotation, in the manufacturer’s words: “You may inject in the same body area, but make sure it is not in the same spot as used the last time.”

Source: emc / Novo Nordisk: Wegovy Patient Information Leaflet, revised 06/2026. Checked 20 September 2026.

European Union

Areas listed:

  • Stomach area (abdomen) — keep at least 5 cm from your belly button
  • Thigh (Upper leg)
  • Back of upper arm — another person should inject here

Rotation, in the manufacturer’s words: “You may inject in the same body area, but make sure it is not in the same spot as used the last time. Avoid using the exact same spot as the last time you injected.”

Source: EMA: Wegovy EPAR Product Information, revised EMA last updated 26/08/2026. Checked 20 September 2026.

Also consulted:

  • EMA: Wegovy: EPAR (European Public Assessment Report) page, listing the current product information and its last-updated date — Used to confirm the current English product information.

United States

Areas listed:

  • Stomach area (abdomen) — keep at least 2 inches from your belly button
  • Thigh (Upper leg)
  • Back of upper arm — another person should inject here

Rotation, in the manufacturer’s words: “Change (rotate) your injection site with each injection. You may inject in the same body area each week, but make sure it is not in the same spot each time.”

Source: FDA / DailyMed: Wegovy prescribing information and patient instructions (DailyMed, current label), revised v19 (2026-06-18). Checked 20 September 2026.

Wegovy dose steps and missed doses

Most GLP-1 medicines start low and step up, which doctors call titration. This is what the Wegovy product information says, quoted word for word. UK wording comes from the patient leaflet; US wording from the prescribing information and Medication Guide. Your prescriber’s instructions come first, and GLPMGR never works out or recommends a dose.

United Kingdom

Dose steps, in the product information’s words:

  • “When you first start using wegovy®, the starting dose is 0.25 mg once weekly.”
  • “Your doctor will instruct you to gradually increase your dose every 4 weeks until you reach the dose of 2.4 mg once weekly.”
  • “Week 1–4: 0.25 mg. Week 5–8: 0.5 mg. Week 9–12: 1 mg. Week 13–16: 1.7 mg. From week 17: 2.4 mg.”
  • “For weight management in patients with obesity: If needed, a dose increase to the next maintenance dose 7.2 mg once weekly (3 injections of 2.4 mg) can be made after a minimum of 4 weeks on 2.4 mg. The maximum dose is 7.2 mg once weekly.”

If a dose is missed:

  • “If you forgot to inject a dose and: it is 5 days or less since you should have used wegovy®, use it as soon as you remember. Then inject your next dose as usual on your scheduled day.”
  • “If you forgot to inject a dose and: it is more than 5 days since you should have used wegovy®, skip the missed dose. Then inject your next dose as usual on your next scheduled day.”
  • “Do not take a double dose to make up for a forgotten dose.”

Using it with another GLP-1 medicine: “Do not use wegovy® in combination with other medicines that contain GLP-1 receptor agonists (such as liraglutide, dulaglutide, exenatide or lixisenatide).”

Source: emc / Novo Nordisk: Wegovy FlexTouch Patient Information Leaflet, revised 08/2026. Checked 27 September 2026.

United States

Dose steps, in the product information’s words:

  • “The recommended starting dosage of WEGOVY injection is 0.25 mg administered subcutaneously once weekly.”
  • “Weeks 1 through 4: 0.25 mg. Weeks 5 through 8: 0.5 mg. Weeks 9 through 12: 1 mg. Weeks 13 through 16: 1.7 mg. Week 17 and onward: maintenance dosage.”
  • “The maintenance dosage of WEGOVY for weight reduction in adults is either 1.7 mg or 2.4 mg (recommended) injected subcutaneously once weekly.”
  • “For patients who tolerate the 2.4 mg dosage for at least 4 weeks and additional weight reduction is clinically indicated, the dosage may be increased to a maximum dosage of 7.2 mg subcutaneously once weekly.”

If a dose is missed:

  • “If you miss a dose of WEGOVY and the next scheduled dose is more than 2 days away (48 hours), take the missed dose as soon as possible. If you miss a dose of WEGOVY and the next scheduled dose is less than 2 days away (48 hours), do not administer the dose. Take your next dose on the regularly scheduled day.”
  • “If you miss doses of WEGOVY for 2 or more consecutive weeks, contact your healthcare provider to talk about how to restart your treatment.”

Using it with another GLP-1 medicine: “Concomitant use of WEGOVY (semaglutide) tablets or WEGOVY (semaglutide) injection with other semaglutide-containing products or with any other GLP-1 receptor agonist is not recommended.”

Switching between the injection and the tablet:

  • “Patients taking WEGOVY 2.4 mg injection for cardiovascular risk reduction or weight reduction in adults may switch to WEGOVY 25 mg tablets.”
  • “One week after discontinuing WEGOVY 2.4 mg injection, initiate 25 mg of WEGOVY tablets orally once daily.”
  • “The day after discontinuing WEGOVY tablets 25 mg once daily, initiate WEGOVY 2.4 mg subcutaneous injection once weekly.”

Source: FDA / Novo Nordisk: Wegovy prescribing information and Medication Guide (injection and tablets), DailyMed, effective 06/2026. Checked 27 September 2026.
