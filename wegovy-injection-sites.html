Wegovy injection sites: what the manufacturer says

Wegovy (semaglutide), checked against the documents below on 20 September 2026

This page lists where the manufacturer says Wegovy (semaglutide) can be injected in the UK, the EU and the US, in their own words, with the documents each list comes from. It is not medical advice.

Talk to a healthcare professional. The injection areas shown come from the manufacturer's product information, and the shading shows where you've injected before. GLPMGR is not a medical device and this is not medical advice: it can't tell you where it is safe for you to inject. Talk to your doctor, pharmacist or nurse about where and how to inject, follow the instructions from your prescriber and the leaflet supplied with your medicine, and ask a healthcare professional if you are unsure, or if you have pain, lumps, bruising or any other problem at an injection site.

Where Wegovy can be injected

Manufacturer: Novo Nordisk

United Kingdom

Areas listed:

  • Abdomen (Stomach) — keep at least 5 cm from your belly button
  • Thigh (Upper leg)
  • Upper arm

Rotation, in the manufacturer’s words: “You may inject in the same body area, but make sure it is not in the same spot as used the last time.”

Source: emc / Novo Nordisk: Wegovy Patient Information Leaflet, revised 06/2026. Checked 20 September 2026.

European Union

Areas listed:

  • Stomach area (abdomen) — keep at least 5 cm from your belly button
  • Thigh (Upper leg)
  • Back of upper arm — another person should inject here

Rotation, in the manufacturer’s words: “You may inject in the same body area, but make sure it is not in the same spot as used the last time. Avoid using the exact same spot as the last time you injected.”

Source: EMA: Wegovy EPAR Product Information, revised EMA last updated 26/08/2026. Checked 20 September 2026.

Also consulted:

  • EMA: Wegovy: EPAR (European Public Assessment Report) page, listing the current product information and its last-updated date — Used to confirm the current English product information.

United States

Areas listed:

  • Stomach area (abdomen) — keep at least 2 inches from your belly button
  • Thigh (Upper leg)
  • Back of upper arm — another person should inject here

Rotation, in the manufacturer’s words: “Change (rotate) your injection site with each injection. You may inject in the same body area each week, but make sure it is not in the same spot each time.”

Source: FDA / DailyMed: Wegovy prescribing information and patient instructions (DailyMed, current label), revised v19 (2026-06-18). Checked 20 September 2026.
