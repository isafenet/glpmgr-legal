GLP-1 tablets

GLP-1 pill tracker: Foundayo, the Wegovy pill and Rybelsus

Leaflets checked 1 October 2026

GLP-1 medicines now come as a daily tablet as well as an injection. GLPMGR tracks them the same way it tracks injections: each tablet, your dose steps, reminders and how you’re feeling. Each tablet has its own rules about how to take it, so below they are in each leaflet’s own words, with a link to the document. This page is not medical advice.

Talk to a healthcare professional. The instructions below are quoted from each medicine’s patient leaflet. Always follow what your doctor, nurse or pharmacist tells you, and read the leaflet that comes with your medicine.

Track your GLP-1 tablets in GLPMGR

  • Log each tablet with its dose, date and time in a few taps on iPhone or iPad, or tap Log Dose on the reminder on your Apple Watch.
  • Enter the dose steps your prescriber gave you, and GLPMGR fills in the right dose each time.
  • See how to take your tablet, from its leaflet, on the dose form, with a link to the source.
  • A countdown for any wait before eating that the leaflet gives.
  • Reminders when your next tablet is due, and a note of side effects in a couple of taps.
  • Moved from injections to tablets? Change your medicine and GLPMGR marks the change.

There’s no account to create, and your data stays in your control. GLPMGR is independent and isn’t affiliated with any manufacturer. Download on the App Store

Foundayo (orforglipron)

Manufacturer: Eli Lilly. Active ingredient: orforglipron.

United Kingdom

We don’t hold a checked United Kingdom leaflet for this medicine (it may not be sold there), so GLPMGR shows no instructions for it.

United States

How to take it, in the leaflet’s words:

  • “Take Foundayo by mouth 1 time each day, with or without food.”
  • “Swallow tablets whole. Do not break, crush, or chew the tablet.”
  • “Do not take more than 1 tablet per day.”

If a dose is missed, the leaflet says: “If you miss a dose, take it as soon as possible. Do not take 2 doses of Foundayo in the same day.”

Wait before eating: The leaflet gives no wait before eating, so GLPMGR shows no countdown.

Source: FDA / Eli Lilly: Foundayo Medication Guide, issued 07/2026. Checked 26 September 2026.

Foundayo dose steps

Quoted from the product information. Your prescriber’s instructions come first.

United Kingdom

We don’t hold checked United Kingdom information for this medicine (it may not be sold there), so nothing is shown.

United States

Dose steps, in the product information’s words:

  • “The starting dosage is 0.8 mg orally once daily. After at least 30 days on the 0.8 mg dosage, increase the dosage to 2.5 mg once daily.”
  • “After at least 30 days on the 2.5 mg dosage, increase the dosage to 5.5 mg once daily.”
  • “The dosage may be increased to the next dosage level (9 mg, 14.5 mg, or 17.2 mg once daily) after at least 30 days on the current dosage, based on treatment response and tolerability.”
  • “The maximum dosage of FOUNDAYO is 17.2 mg once daily.”

Source: Eli Lilly: Foundayo prescribing information and Medication Guide (US), 2026. Checked 27 September 2026.

Wegovy pill (semaglutide)

Manufacturer: Novo Nordisk. Active ingredient: semaglutide.

United Kingdom

We don’t hold a checked United Kingdom leaflet for this medicine (it may not be sold there), so GLPMGR shows no instructions for it.

United States

How to take it, in the leaflet’s words:

  • “Take 1 Wegovy tablet by mouth on an empty stomach in the morning with water (no more than 4 ounces). Do not take Wegovy tablets with any other liquids besides water.”
  • “Do not split, crush, chew or dissolve Wegovy tablets. Swallow Wegovy tablets whole.”
  • “Do not take more than 1 Wegovy tablet each day.”
  • “After 30 minutes, you can eat, drink, or take other oral medicines.”

If a dose is missed, the leaflet says: “If you miss a dose of Wegovy, skip the missed dose and go back to your regular schedule for the next dose.”

Wait before eating: GLPMGR counts down the 30-minute wait after you log a tablet.

Source: FDA / Novo Nordisk: Wegovy Medication Guide (Wegovy tablets), revised 12/2025. Checked 26 September 2026.

Wegovy pill dose steps

Quoted from the product information. Your prescriber’s instructions come first.

United Kingdom

We don’t hold checked United Kingdom information for this medicine (it may not be sold there), so nothing is shown.

United States

Dose steps, in the product information’s words:

  • “The recommended starting dosage of WEGOVY tablets is 1.5 mg taken orally once daily.”
  • “Days 1 through 30: 1.5 mg. Days 31 through 60: 4 mg. Days 61 through 90: 9 mg. Day 91 and onward (maintenance): 25 mg.”
  • “If patients do not tolerate a dose during dosage escalation, consider delaying dosage escalation.”

Using it with another GLP-1 medicine: “Concomitant use of WEGOVY (semaglutide) tablets or WEGOVY (semaglutide) injection with other semaglutide-containing products or with any other GLP-1 receptor agonist is not recommended.”

Switching between the injection and the tablet:

  • “Patients taking WEGOVY 2.4 mg injection for cardiovascular risk reduction or weight reduction in adults may switch to WEGOVY 25 mg tablets.”
  • “One week after discontinuing WEGOVY 2.4 mg injection, initiate 25 mg of WEGOVY tablets orally once daily.”
  • “The day after discontinuing WEGOVY tablets 25 mg once daily, initiate WEGOVY 2.4 mg subcutaneous injection once weekly.”

Source: FDA / Novo Nordisk: Wegovy prescribing information and Medication Guide (injection and tablets), DailyMed, effective 06/2026. Checked 27 September 2026.

Rybelsus (semaglutide)

Manufacturer: Novo Nordisk. Active ingredient: semaglutide.

United Kingdom

How to take it, in the leaflet’s words:

  • “Take your Rybelsus tablet on an empty stomach after a recommended fasting period of at least 8 hours.”
  • “Swallow your Rybelsus tablet whole with a sip of water (up to 120 mL).”
  • “Do not split, crush or chew the tablet, as it is not known if it affects absorption of semaglutide.”
  • “After taking your Rybelsus tablet wait at least 30 minutes before eating, drinking or taking other oral medicines.”

If a dose is missed, the leaflet says: “If you forget to take a dose, skip the missed dose and just take your normal dose the next day.”

Wait before eating: GLPMGR counts down the 30-minute wait after you log a tablet.

Source: MHRA / Novo Nordisk: Rybelsus 1.5, 4 and 9 mg package leaflet (PLGB 04668/0451-0453; the 3, 7 and 14 mg leaflet says the same), revised 11/2025. Checked 1 October 2026.

United States

How to take it, in the leaflet’s words:

  • “Take 1 Rybelsus tablet by mouth on an empty stomach in the morning with water (no more than 4 ounces). Do not take Rybelsus tablets with any other liquids besides water.”
  • “Do not split, crush, chew, or dissolve Rybelsus tablets. Swallow Rybelsus tablets whole.”
  • “After 30 minutes, you can eat, drink, or take other oral medicines.”
  • “Do not take more than 1 Rybelsus tablet each day.”

If a dose is missed, the leaflet says: “If you miss a dose of Rybelsus, skip the missed dose and go back to your regular schedule.”

Wait before eating: GLPMGR counts down the 30-minute wait after you log a tablet.

Source: FDA / Novo Nordisk: Rybelsus and Ozempic tablets Medication Guide, revised 1/2026. Checked 26 September 2026.

Rybelsus dose steps

Quoted from the product information. Your prescriber’s instructions come first.

United Kingdom

Dose steps, in the product information’s words:

  • “The starting dose is one 3 mg tablet once a day for one month. After one month, your doctor will increase your dose to one 7 mg tablet once a day.”
  • “Your doctor will instruct you to stay on a dose for minimum one month before increasing to a higher dose. Your doctor may increase your dose to one 14 mg tablet once a day if your blood sugar is not controlled well enough with a dose of 7 mg once a day.”
  • “1.5, 4 and 9 mg tablets: The starting dose is one 1.5 mg tablet once a day for one month. After one month, your doctor will increase your dose to one 4 mg tablet once a day. Your doctor may increase your dose to one 9 mg tablet once a day if your blood sugar is not controlled well enough with a dose of 4 mg once a day.”
  • “Rybelsus® should always be taken as one tablet per day. You should not take two tablets to get the effect of a higher dose.”

Source: MHRA / Novo Nordisk: Rybelsus package leaflets (3, 7 and 14 mg, PLGB 04668/0420-0422; 1.5, 4 and 9 mg, PLGB 04668/0451-0453), revised 11/2025. Checked 1 October 2026.

United States

Dose steps, in the product information’s words:

  • “Day 1 to 30: Recommended starting dosage is 3 mg orally once daily for 30 days (this dosage is not effective for glycemic control).”
  • “Days 31 to 60: Increase the dosage to 7 mg orally once daily.”
  • “On Day 61 or thereafter, if: No additional glycemic control is needed, maintain the dosage at 7 mg orally once daily. Additional glycemic control is needed, increase the dosage to 14 mg orally once daily.”

Source: FDA / Novo Nordisk: Rybelsus and Ozempic tablets prescribing information and Medication Guide, revised 1/2026. Checked 27 September 2026.

Questions people ask

Can I track orforglipron (Foundayo) in GLPMGR?

Yes. Choose Orforglipron (Foundayo) when you set up your medicine, add the dose steps your prescriber gave you, and log each tablet in a few taps. GLPMGR shows the Medication Guide's wording on how to take it and reminds you when it's due.

Does GLPMGR work for the Wegovy pill and Rybelsus?

Yes. Both are semaglutide as a tablet, and GLPMGR tracks them the same way: each tablet, your dose steps and reminders, with each leaflet's wording on how to take it.

Is orforglipron the same as semaglutide?

No. Rybelsus and the Wegovy pill are semaglutide as a tablet. Foundayo (orforglipron) is a different GLP-1 medicine that only comes as a tablet.

Does GLPMGR tell me when I can eat after my tablet?

Where the leaflet gives a wait before eating, GLPMGR counts it down after you log the tablet: 30 minutes for the Wegovy pill and Rybelsus, in the countries whose leaflet we've checked. The Foundayo Medication Guide says it can be taken with or without food, so there's no countdown for it.

Does GLPMGR tell me what dose to take, or what to do if I miss one?

No. GLPMGR follows the dose steps you enter and shows the leaflet's own words about missed doses. It never works out or recommends a dose. Always follow your doctor, nurse or pharmacist.

Can I switch from injections to tablets?

Yes. If your prescriber moves you from an injection to a tablet, change your medicine in GLPMGR. It checks it's the one you meant and marks the change in your history and your report.
