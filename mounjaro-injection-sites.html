Mounjaro injection sites: what the manufacturer says

Mounjaro (tirzepatide), checked against the documents below on 20 September 2026

This page lists where the manufacturer says Mounjaro (tirzepatide) can be injected in the UK, the EU and the US, in their own words, with the documents each list comes from. It is not medical advice.

Talk to a healthcare professional. The injection areas shown come from the manufacturer's product information, and the heat map is your own record. GLPMGR is not a medical device and this is not medical advice: it can't tell you where it is safe for you to inject. Talk to your doctor, pharmacist or nurse about where and how to inject, follow the instructions from your prescriber and the leaflet supplied with your medicine, and ask a healthcare professional if you are unsure, or if you have pain, lumps, bruising or any other problem at an injection site.

Where Mounjaro can be injected

Manufacturer: Eli Lilly

United Kingdom

Areas listed:

  • Stomach area (abdomen) — keep at least 5 cm from your belly button
  • Upper leg (thigh)
  • Back of upper arm — another person should inject here

Rotation, in the manufacturer’s words: “Change (rotate) your injection site each week. You may use the same area of your body but be sure to choose a different injection site in that area.”

Source: emc / Eli Lilly: Mounjaro KwikPen Instructions for Use. Checked 20 September 2026.

European Union

Areas listed:

  • Stomach area (abdomen) — keep at least 5 cm from your belly button
  • Upper leg (thigh)
  • Back of upper arm — another person should inject here

Rotation, in the manufacturer’s words: “Change (rotate) your injection site each week. You may use the same area of your body, but be sure to choose a different injection site in that area.”

Source: EMA: Mounjaro EPAR Product Information (English). Checked 20 September 2026.

United States

Areas listed:

  • Stomach area (abdomen) — keep at least 2 inches from your belly button
  • Thigh
  • Back of upper arm — another person should inject here

Rotation, in the manufacturer’s words: “Change (rotate) your injection site with each weekly injection.”

Source: FDA / DailyMed: Mounjaro prescribing information and patient instructions (DailyMed, current label), revised v40 (2026-08-27). Checked 20 September 2026.
