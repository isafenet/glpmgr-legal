2 What GLPMGR is

GLPMGR is a personal tracking app for people on GLP-1 medication regimens. It helps you log injections, weight, symptoms, and body measurements, view progress over time, and set dose reminders. GLPMGR is free to use. Some features need GLPMGR Premium, an optional subscription (see section 5); everything else stays free.

Always follow your prescribing clinician's instructions, and consult your doctor or pharmacist before making any change to your treatment. If you believe you're experiencing a medical emergency, contact emergency services immediately rather than relying on this app.
