2 What GLPMGR is

GLPMGR is a personal tracking app for people on GLP-1 medication regimens. It helps you log injections, weight, symptoms, and body measurements, view progress over time, and set dose reminders. Some features — such as extended history, additional charts, and unlimited photo tracking — are available only with a GLPMGR Premium subscription.
