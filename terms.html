2 What GLPMGR is

GLPMGR is a personal tracking app for people on GLP-1 medication regimens, on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. It helps you log injections and tablets, dose steps, weight, symptoms, water, protein, blood glucose and body measurements, see your progress over time, and set reminders. It is for your personal, non-commercial use. GLPMGR is free to use. Some features need GLPMGR Premium, an optional subscription (see section 5); everything else stays free.

3 Not medical advice

GLPMGR is a record-keeping and reminder tool. It is not a medical device. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and nobody at GLPMGR acts as your doctor, nurse or pharmacist. GLPMGR never works out or recommends a dose. Nothing in the app, including dose reminders, charts, estimates or projections, should be treated as a recommendation about what, when or how much medication to take.

Figures such as the estimated medication level chart are estimates worked out from what you log. They are not measurements of the medicine in your body. Information about injection areas comes from the manufacturer's product information; it can't tell you where it is safe for you to inject.

  • Follow your prescriber. Always follow the instructions of your prescriber and pharmacist, and the leaflet supplied with your medicine. Talk to your doctor or pharmacist before you make any change to your treatment.
  • Check what you log. Reminders and charts are only as accurate as what you enter. If a reminder or chart disagrees with your prescription or your medicine's instructions, follow your prescription and the instructions.
  • Get advice when unsure. If you have side effects that worry you, or you're unsure whether you have taken the right dose, contact your doctor or pharmacist straight away (in the UK you can also call NHS 111).
  • Emergencies. GLPMGR is not for medical emergencies. If you think you need urgent medical help, call your local emergency number (999 in the UK) straight away rather than relying on this app.

This section explains what GLPMGR can and can't do so you can make informed choices. It does not remove our responsibility where we have been negligent (see section 11).
