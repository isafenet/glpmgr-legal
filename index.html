Features

Everything for your routine. Nothing you don’t need.

GLPMGR is built around how people actually take GLP-1 medicine: remembering when it’s due, stepping up doses, where you inject and how you’re feeling. New to some of the words? We explain them in plain English.

Doses & dose steps

Log every injection or tablet in seconds. Most GLP-1 medicines start low and step up over a few weeks (doctors call this titration). Enter the steps your prescriber gave you and GLPMGR fills in the right dose each time, up to the dose you’ll stay on.

Injection areas from your medicine’s instructions

GLPMGR only offers the injection areas your medicine’s manufacturer lists for your region, and shows where you have injected on a body map. If we don’t hold a verified list for a medicine, it doesn’t offer a site. This is not medical advice: talk to your doctor, pharmacist or nurse about where and how to inject. See where the guidance comes from.

Reminders & supply alerts

Get reminded before each dose is due, plus a heads-up when your pen, vial or prescription is running low.

Weight, goals & Apple Health

Log weight in kilograms, pounds or stones, and set a goal if you’d like one. Every body responds in its own way and at its own pace, so progress is shown gently, with no judgement. Apple Health is off until you turn it on.

A symptom diary that helps

Side effects like nausea or tiredness are common, and never something you’re doing wrong. Note how you feel in a couple of taps and see when it tends to happen after a dose, ready to talk through with your doctor or nurse.

Estimated medication level

A gentle chart of roughly how much medicine may be in your body between doses, worked out from the doses you log and how long the medicine usually lasts. An estimate for context, not a blood test.

Photos & measurements

Keep body measurements and progress photos alongside your weight if you want to. Photos stay on your device, locked behind Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

Share with your doctor or nurse

Create a tidy PDF report to take to appointments, or a spreadsheet of your entries. A password-protected backup lets you move to a new device. Every file is made on your device.

FAQ

Good questions

Is there an app to track GLP-1 injections?

Yes. GLPMGR is a private GLP-1 tracker for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. It records each injection with its dose and injection site, follows your dose steps (titration), tracks weight and side effects, and reminds you when your next dose is due. It needs no account and keeps your data on your device.

Which GLP-1 medicines can I track?

Semaglutide (including Ozempic and Wegovy), tirzepatide (Mounjaro and Zepbound), liraglutide (Saxenda and Victoza), dulaglutide (Trulicity) and exenatide (Byetta and Bydureon) as injections, and semaglutide tablets (Rybelsus). You can also type in any other medicine by name. Injection-site information is shown only for medicines with a verified manufacturer list.

Can I use GLPMGR as a Mounjaro, Ozempic, Wegovy or Zepbound tracker?

Yes. Choose the medicine when you record a dose, set your schedule and dose steps, and GLPMGR tracks the rest. GLPMGR is an independent app and is not affiliated with any manufacturer; brand names are used only to say which medicines it works with.

Does GLPMGR tell me where to inject?

No. It shows the injection areas the manufacturer lists for your medicine in your region, and marks on a body map where you have injected before. It does not recommend a site, and it is not medical advice. Talk to your doctor, pharmacist or nurse about where and how to inject.

How does GLPMGR help with injection site rotation?

Each injection can be recorded against one of the listed areas. The body map shades areas by how often you have used them, so you can see your own history at a glance. Where the manufacturer gives rotation wording, GLPMGR shows it word for word.

Where can I see the manufacturer's injection sites for my medicine?

On our injection site guidance pages, one for each medicine. Each shows the areas the manufacturer lists in the UK, EU and US, any rule that another person should inject an area or that you keep a distance from your belly button, the manufacturer's rotation wording, and links to the documents.

Does GLPMGR support dose steps (titration)?

Yes. Most GLP-1 medicines start at a low dose and step up over a few weeks, which doctors call titration. Enter the steps your prescriber gave you and GLPMGR fills in the right dose whenever you log an injection, up to your maintenance dose (the dose you stay on). It never works out or recommends a dose.

Can I track my weight in kilograms, pounds or stones?

Yes. You can log weight in kilograms, pounds or stones, and set a goal if you want one. Weight can also sync with Apple Health, which is off until you turn it on.
