The headline

Your new daily check-in.

Appetite, energy, sleep and bowel movements in a few taps, from a new tile on Today. Then see your answers week by week in Symptom Trends, free. With Premium, see them across the days after each dose.

  • A few taps, once a day.
  • Stays on your iPhone, like everything else in GLPMGR.
  • Spot how you feel on dose day compared with the days after.

Built around accessibility

Charts you can hear.

With Audio Graphs, VoiceOver plays your weight trend as rising and falling tones. Text contrast is stronger everywhere, and layouts adapt at the largest text sizes.
